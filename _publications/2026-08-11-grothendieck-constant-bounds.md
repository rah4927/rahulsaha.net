abstract: 'We establish new lower and upper bounds for the Grothendieck constant $K_G$, which captures the hardness between combinatorial problems and their continuous relaxations. Through a combination of novel analytical techniques and computational verification, we tighten the best known bounds to $6\pi/11 \leq K_G \leq \pi/(2\log(1+\sqrt{2})) - 10^{-4}$. These improvements represent significant progress on a long-standing open problem in functional analysis and theoretical computer science.'
