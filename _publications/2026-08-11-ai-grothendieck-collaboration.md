abstract: 'We present an extensive case study of how AI was used to improve bounds on the Grothendieck constant $K_G$, a fundamental constant in functional analysis that captures the hardness between combinatorial problems and their continuous relaxations. Through a novel long-horizon AI research system capable of extended mathematical reasoning, we achieved improvements to bounds that had remained essentially unchanged for decades. The system demonstrated the ability to generate insights deemed novel by domain experts, offering a glimpse into the potential of AI-assisted mathematical research. This work represents a significant milestone in human-AI collaboration for advancing theoretical mathematics.'
